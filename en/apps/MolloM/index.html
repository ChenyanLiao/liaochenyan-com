01

Setting up a calculation

MolloM gives supported docking and MD tools a graphical interface. Set up a run for your computer or SSH server, then inspect its results and logs in the same project.

  1. 01

    Get the tools ready

    The software centre detects existing tools and offers installation, version management and checks for supported integrations. Choose your computer or an SSH server, inspect inputs, and place the docking box in 3D. Installation still depends on the OS, licence and available packages.

  2. 02

    Keep the parameters within reach

    Engines have their own controls: search effort, seeds, CPU and GPU options, plus GROMACS runtime and MDP settings. Dedicated controls and native-protocol entries preserve detailed configuration for review before submission.

  3. 03

    Handle a batch without starting over

    Use a small trial to estimate runtime before allocating concurrency and resources. Choose engines and filters by round; retry failed items without repeating successful work. Keep candidate provenance across rounds and page through results and logs. Throughput still needs testing on your hardware and data.

02

Desktop and mobile

Desktop

Connect your computer or an SSH server, check receptors and ligands, set a 3D docking box, and choose an engine, parameters and resources. Estimate runtime with a trial, follow progress and logs, filter candidates into another round, or hand a pose to GROMACS and inspect trajectories and analyses. Researchers choose the engine and parameters for each round.

Mobile

Read projects, progress, result summaries and logs from a connected compute host. Keep notes, favourites, candidate comparisons and timestamped offline copies. For supported desktop projects, edit pending plans and review changes; submit only with separate permission and resource confirmation.

Actual development interfaces with demo settings and simulated tasks. No real computation was submitted.

Mobile is in development. Pairing and selected monitoring have been tested on a local network; public internet access, cellular use and system notifications have not completed public acceptance. Device access requires explicit authorization. The 0.302.0 download is a developer preview and does not include later source changes.

03

Programs and job records

MolloM is not a new docking or MD engine. It calls existing programs and retains original output, parameters and attempts. Project packages carry selected inputs and results. Compatible AI agents can read status or prepare plans through MCP.

04

Where calculations run and data travels

Docking and simulations run on your computer or chosen server. Account connections can relay selected task status, result summaries and logs to your phone. Software installation and external services you enable also use the network.

Interpreting the results

Docking scores and trajectories help compare candidates; they do not establish binding or mechanism on their own. Interpretation still depends on molecular identity, protonation, force fields, sampling and experimental evidence.
