Pro plans

YanMore Grind is free to download. Core recording and dialing do not require a subscription. When purchasing is enabled, check the plan, actual price, billing period, offers and renewal conditions in the app and at Google Play checkout. Full backup export is free; full import or restoration requires Pro. Restoring purchases does not restore coffee records.

Google Play Billing and purchase verification

If auto-renewal is enabled, manage or cancel it in Google Play. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Use the original Google account to restore purchases. Refunds follow Google Play’s rules and applicable law. Google Play Subscriptions · Google Play Refunds
