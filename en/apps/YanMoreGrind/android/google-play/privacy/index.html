For YanMore Grind on Google Play. These pages describe the features and payment design reviewed so far. Pro products and production purchase verification are not active. Third-party data handling and server retention still need to be confirmed before the paid release. Do not use these pages as the final store-submission policy or terms.

Information on your device

Beans, equipment, recipes, brews, tasting and consumption records, photos, optional sleep self-assessments, and data used to adapt recommendations are stored on your device. You enter sleep assessments yourself; the app does not read them from health apps or require a YanMore account. System language and time zone are used for display.

Photos, reminders and sharing

Bean-bag text recognition processes selected images on the device. When the app stores copies of selected photos, it re-encodes them without copying the original EXIF location. System pickers or external apps can handle files or camera capture. Brew prompt text is passed to your system speech engine; the app selects a voice marked as offline or skips speech if none is available. Notifications and exact alarms support local reminders. Copied text goes to the system clipboard. Services you select handle items you choose to share or export under their own rules.

Google Play Billing and purchase verification

The Play inspection build contains Google Play Billing 8.3.0. In the reviewed design, Google Play handles payment after configuration and after you agree to connect. When the app checks or refreshes an existing entitlement, or when you buy, restore or check a pending order, the app sends the package name, product ID, purchase token, random installation ID and request nonce to the developer’s verification service, which checks the order with Google. Coffee records, photos and sleep assessments are not sent with these requests. Verification has been tested locally; no production service has been confirmed. Google Privacy

Retention and deletion

Manage local records on your device. Archiving does not delete them, and exported backups must be deleted separately. In the verification design, the cached entitlement is valid for up to 24 hours on the device, and the revocation index holds digests of one-time purchase tokens or subscription order numbers. The 24-hour period is not a server retention limit. Production regions and retention and deletion rules for logs, the index and support email are not yet confirmed.
