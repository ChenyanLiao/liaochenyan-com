Android terms of use

Updated November 18, 2026

These terms apply only to the generic Android 3.0.0 build without payments. It does not offer store purchases or purchase restoration. Before a payment-enabled version is released, product, price and channel terms will be updated.

Using the app

YanMore Grind helps you record beans, equipment, recipes and brews. Installing it does not transfer intellectual property in the software or its original content; open-source components have their own licences. You retain the relevant rights in your notes and photos and should have permission to import and share content.

Recommendations and estimates

Grind recommendations use equipment, recipes and feedback to inform your next adjustment; they do not guarantee accuracy, successful cups or a particular flavour. A target is not an actual measurement. The app does not directly control your coffee machine or grinder or replace its instructions and safety checks. Caffeine and other component figures are estimates, not laboratory results or medical diagnoses.

Records and backups

The basic tier retains core recording and dialing workflows. The most recent 20 records are a display limit; older records are not automatically deleted solely because that limit is exceeded. Full backup export does not require Pro; full import or restoration requires Pro access. The generic no-payment build does not offer a way to purchase Pro.

Keep a backup before changing devices, importing or troubleshooting; do not routinely uninstall or clear app data to investigate a problem. Recent workbench settings and retained professional workbench drafts are not included in the current full backup. Backup format support depends on the actual app version.
