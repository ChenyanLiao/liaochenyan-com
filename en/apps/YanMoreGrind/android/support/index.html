Android support

Questions about YanMore Grind? Here are a few places to start.

Keep a backup

Before reinstalling the app, clearing its data or moving to a new phone, export a backup using the app’s data tools. Check that it includes photos if you need them, and keep the original device’s data until you have checked your records on the new device.

Exporting a full backup is available without Pro. Importing or restoring a full backup requires Pro access.

Recent workbench settings and retained professional workbench drafts are not included in the current full backup. Saved recipes and brew records are separate from these drafts.

Recording a brew

Use a consistent unit for your grinder settings. Enter the target yield and time for the recipe, then record the actual yield and time measured for the cup. Check the selected bean and equipment when you change your setup.

Notifications and reminders

If a reminder does not appear, check notification permissions and the phone’s background and battery settings. Some phones may delay reminders when the app is in the background.

Contact

Email Liao_Chenyan@yeah.net and include your phone model, Android version, app version, the steps you took and what happened. A relevant screenshot can help.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information or a complete personal backup.
