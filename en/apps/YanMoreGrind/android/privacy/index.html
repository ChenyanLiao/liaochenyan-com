Android privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026

This notice applies only to the generic Android 3.0.0 build without payments. It does not apply to builds integrating Google Play Billing, Huawei IAP or other store payment components. The relevant notice will be reviewed and updated before a payment-enabled version is released.

YanMore Grind is a coffee dialing and record-keeping app. The developer is Chenyan Liao; contact Liao_Chenyan@yeah.net with privacy questions.

Information you save

You may enter beans, equipment, recipes, brew results, tasting notes, consumption and milk records, photos, sleep self-assessments and notes. The app also saves settings and local learning results. These are stored in app-private storage on your device. Self-assessments are entered by you, not read from other health apps. No YanMore account is required.

The app uses system language, time zone, fonts and window dimensions for display. App-private storage does not imply additional database encryption.

Photos, text recognition and speech

You can select photos or files using system pickers, or use an external camera app. Saved images and thumbnails are re-encoded without copying original EXIF location metadata. Original images and copies held by external apps remain under your and those providers' control.

Bean-bag text recognition processes selected images on the device using a bundled model; you can review the result. The origin map uses recorded origins, not device location. Spoken brew prompts are sent as text to the system speech engine; the app selects an installed voice marked as not requiring a network and skips speech if none is available. The engine and external camera are managed by their providers.

Permissions, reminders and network access

Notifications provide local brew-timer and milk reminders; lock-screen display depends on system settings. When permitted, reminders can be restored after device startup or time and time-zone changes. Exact alarms are used for timed prompts; without permission, inexact reminders may be delayed.

The app declares internet, network-state, wake-lock, foreground-service and internal receiver permissions. Your browser and network providers process requests when you open support or privacy webpages. Text you copy enters the system clipboard. Runtime data processing by third-party components in the final release remains to be checked; do not apply this notice to other builds.

Sharing, backup and deletion

You initiate sharing and export. A full backup may contain record IDs, equipment associations, original snapshots and photos. Your chosen email, messaging, file or cloud service handles shared content under its own terms. Full backup export does not require Pro; full import or restoration requires Pro access. Recent workbench settings and retained professional workbench drafts are not included in the current full backup.

The current app configuration excludes its data from system backup and device migration; keep your own exported backup. App data remains on the device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app; delete exported backups yourself. Archiving differs from permanent deletion. Temporary files are generally cleaned up when possible, but immediate cleanup after errors is not guaranteed. Email and attachments are handled by the sender's and recipient's mail providers; contact us about inquiries and deletion requests.

Third-party components and payments

Static dependency checks of the generic build include Tesseract4Android 4.9.0 and AndroidX Window 1.5.0. This build does not include Huawei IAP or Google Play Billing and does not offer store purchases, purchase restoration or payment verification. System pickers, speech engines, external cameras and sharing services you choose may be provided by third parties and are governed by their providers.

Runtime networking and data processing by all third-party components have not yet been fully verified. This notice describes the currently known scope and is not a complete privacy disclosure for payment-enabled builds.

Contact

For questions about data access, correction or deletion, email Liao_Chenyan@yeah.net. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary complete backups. This notice will be updated as released versions and data practices change.
